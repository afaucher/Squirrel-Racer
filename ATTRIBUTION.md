# Attribution

Squirrel Ranger is built on public sources. This page lists them all.

All artwork, animation and sound in the game is original and generated in code. No outside images are used. The species facts, ranges and field marks below were researched from these public references and rewritten in our own words.

## Species references

The common names, scientific names, native ranges, facts and field marks on every card were checked against these references in October 2026. Each claim cites its own sources in `data/*.js`, and the tables below list every source used for each species.

- [All About Birds](https://www.allaboutbirds.org), Cornell Lab of Ornithology: North American bird ranges, field marks and calls.
- [Audubon Guide to North American Birds](https://www.audubon.org/bird-guide) and Cornell Lab's [Birds of the World](https://birdsoftheworld.org) (free overview pages): bird ranges, field marks and behaviour.
- [RSPB A–Z of UK birds](https://www.rspb.org.uk/birds-and-wildlife/a-z) and [BTO BirdFacts](https://www.bto.org/understanding-birds/birdfacts): British and European birds.
- [Lady Bird Johnson Wildflower Center](https://www.wildflower.org), University of Texas: North American wildflower names and native ranges.
- [Woodland Trust](https://www.woodlandtrust.org.uk), [Plantlife](https://www.plantlife.org.uk) and [EUFORGEN](https://www.euforgen.org): British and European trees and wildflowers.
- [Trees for Life: the Caledonian Forest](https://treesforlife.org.uk/into-the-forest/the-caledonian-forest/) and its [trees, plants and animals](https://treesforlife.org.uk/into-the-forest/trees-plants-animals/): the Scottish pinewood.
- [Fire Effects Information System](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis), USDA Forest Service: North American tree and wildflower ranges.
- Burns & Honkala (coordinators), *Silvics of North America*, USDA Agriculture Handbook 654 (1990). [Volume 1, Conifers](https://research.fs.usda.gov/treesearch/1547).
- [Animal Diversity Web](https://animaldiversity.org), University of Michigan: squirrel ranges and habits (Douglas squirrel, Arizona gray squirrel).
- State and provincial wildlife agencies: [Washington Department of Fish and Wildlife](https://wdfw.wa.gov/species-habitats/species/sciurus-griseus) (western gray squirrel), [South Carolina DNR](https://www.dnr.sc.gov/wildlife/species/foxsquirrel.html) (fox squirrel colour forms), [NC Wildlife](https://www.ncwildlife.org/portals/0/learning/documents/profiles/southflysquirrel.pdf) (southern flying squirrel) and [Alaska Department of Fish and Game](https://newsrelease.adfg.alaska.gov/static/education/wns/northern_flying_squirrel.pdf) (northern flying squirrel).
- [Olympic National Park: Douglas squirrel](https://www.nps.gov/olym/learn/nature/douglas-squirrel-fact-sheet.htm), National Park Service.
- [Burke Herbarium](https://burkeherbarium.org), University of Washington: where Pacific Northwest wildflowers grow.
- [The Gymnosperm Database](https://www.conifers.org) and the [USDA PLANTS plant guides](https://plants.sc.egov.usda.gov): conifer cones.
- The Japanese beech forest: [UNESCO: Shirakami-Sanchi](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/663/), Japan's [Ministry of the Environment](https://www.env.go.jp) (including the [Biodiversity Center of Japan](https://www.biodic.go.jp) bird atlas), the [Forestry Agency's Tohoku office](https://www.rinya.maff.go.jp/tohoku/) (tree pages, beech crop survey and Shirakami monitoring), prefectural Red Data Books (Kyoto, Aichi), [Hokkaido University's plant pages](https://hosho.ees.hokudai.ac.jp/tsuyu/), [Kotobank](https://kotobank.jp) encyclopedia entries and papers on [J-STAGE](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp).
- The Atlantic Forest's araucaria forest: [Embrapa](https://www.embrapa.br) (the *Espécies Arbóreas Brasileiras* tree volumes and Embrapa Florestas), Paraná's environment agency [IAT](https://www.iat.pr.gov.br), ICMBio's journal [Biodiversidade Brasileira](https://revistaeletronica.icmbio.gov.br), the Brazilian Society of Mammalogy's [list of Brazilian mammals](https://zenodo.org/records/18378485) and the [Mammal Diversity Database](https://www.mammaldiversity.org), the [WWF-Brasil Atlantic Forest bird guide](https://d3nehc6yl9qzo4.cloudfront.net/downloads/guia_de_aves_mataatlantica_wwfbrasil.pdf), [Flora e Funga do Brasil](https://floradobrasil.jbrj.gov.br) (through its official checklist on GBIF), and Brazilian papers in [SciELO](https://www.scielo.br), *Biota Neotropica* and *Biotemas*.
- [IOC World Bird List](https://www.worldbirdnames.org): bird names outside North America and Britain.
- [RHS](https://www.rhs.org.uk), [GBIF](https://www.gbif.org) and [World Flora Online](https://www.worldfloraonline.org): current scientific names for plants.
- [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org): species summaries and ranges, under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

These are cited as fact references only; no text or images are copied from them.

## Sounds

The ambience and every bird call are synthesized in the browser; the game uses no recordings. Each call is modelled on how field guides (listed above) describe its rhythm and pitch.
