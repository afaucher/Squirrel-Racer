A cozy field guide you can climb

Squirrel Ranger

Be a real squirrel in five real forests. Run the branches, leap between trees, photograph the birds and wildflowers, and collect the nuts and cones. There's no score and no wrong way to go.

  • MoveLeft thumb, W A S D, or a controller's left stick. In Nimble motion (B), steer anywhere within reach of a branch: the body glides in straight lines while the feet keep hold, so slipping between nearby branches, going under, or around a trunk just happens. In Surface motion (A), run along the branch and push into it to swing to the other side.
  • LeapRight thumb, the arrow keys, or a controller's right stick. Pull back away from where you want to go, then let go. Hold longer to wind up a bigger leap; on keys, the direction is the average of what you've held.
  • Land anywhereYou latch onto whatever you touch: limb, trunk or ground. Thin branch tips bend and weaken your launch, so leap from closer to the trunk.
  • PhotoHold the camera (top right), Space, or a controller's A button to look through the lens. It opens under your finger or mouse pointer and follows it as you slide (on a keyboard or controller, the arrows or right stick aim it). Let go to take the photo. Only what's inside the circle counts, and what your mission is looking for is chosen first. Nuts and cones within reach are collected.
  • MenuThe acorn (top left), M or Start. Play any of five forests as one of its squirrels, take a mission (each is dealt its own forest and squirrel), or browse your collection.
  • MissionsPhotograph three different birds, collect three different nuts or cones, or photograph three different wildflowers. Each mission shows its three in outline on the right, and each one fills in when you find it; tap a frame to see it again (and hear the call, for birds). Finish a mission and the squirrel you played joins your collection.
  • Flying squirrels glidePlay as a southern, northern or Humboldt's flying squirrel and, once a leap starts to fall, it spreads its gliding membrane and sails much farther.
  • Big leapsLong flights turn your tail into a rainbow. Land a long leap on a branch and you get stars, fireflies and falling leaves.
  • Full screenTap the button at the bottom of the play area, or press F. Esc exits.
